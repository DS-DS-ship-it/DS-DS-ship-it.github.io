⏳ How Outbox Persistence Works:

1. When you click "Send Message", your email is stored on your hard drive in your local Outbox.
2. The background engine attempts transmission immediately. If a receiving server (like Yahoo) is temporarily rate-limiting or greylisting, the email stays in your Outbox.
3. The engine automatically retries transmission every 15–30 seconds. Even if you turn off your computer or close Safari, it resumes retrying automatically when you return.
