One-time connection

Connect EmailJS to ZeroMail

Free EmailJS account required. No Worker, server URL, private API key or email password.

  1. Create a free EmailJS account, then open the EmailJS dashboard.
  2. Under Email Services, connect the email account you want ZeroMail to send from and note its Service ID.
  3. Under Email Templates, set To Email to {{to_email}}, Subject to {{subject}}, Reply To to {{reply_to}}, and the message body to {{message}}.
  4. To send uploaded pictures/videos as real attachments, open the template's Attachments tab, add a Form File Attachment, and set its Parameter Name to attachments. Save the template. EmailJS must have attachment support available on your plan.
  5. Open Account in EmailJS and copy your Public Key. This is designed for browser use; do not use your Private Key here.
Important: In EmailJS, add your published ZeroMail website domain to the allowed origins / domain restrictions and enable rate limiting. Never paste an EmailJS Private Key or your Gmail/Outlook password into ZeroMail.
