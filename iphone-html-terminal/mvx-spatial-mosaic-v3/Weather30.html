🏛️ NWS Services & AI Forecasting Briefing

Core Public Weather Services

  • Watches, Warnings, Advisories: Issued by NWS to protect life & property. Warnings indicate immediate threats (e.g. Tornado, Flash Flood).
  • NOAA Weather Radio (NWR): Continuous nationwide 24/7 direct broadcast network.
  • Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA): Targeted cell broadcast emergency text alerts for severe weather warnings.
  • NDFD Gridded Database: Digital GRIB2, XML/SOAP, and GIS spatial feeds compiled from 122 Weather Forecast Offices (WFOs).

Specialized Operations

  • Aviation Weather (AWC): Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts (TAFs & METARs) for flight safety (winds, ceiling, shear).
  • Fire Weather & IMETs: Red Flag Warnings, Spot Forecasts, NFDRS ratings, and on-site Incident Meteorologists deployed to wildfire outbreaks.
  • Space Weather (SWPC): Alerts for solar flares and coronal mass ejections affecting satellites, power grids, and radio signals.

Forecasting Science & AI Models

  • Persistence & Barometric Tendency: Rapid drops (>1.5 hPa) signal approaching storm fronts; rapid rises signal clearing skies.
  • Nowcasting: Short-range 0-6 hour extrapolation using live radar, satellite, and mesoscale numerical models.
  • AI Machine Learning Models:
    • DeepMind GenCast: State-of-the-art probabilistic ensemble predictions.
    • Huawei Pangu-Weather & Google GraphCast: Data-driven 3D weather prediction operating orders-of-magnitude faster than physics models.
    • ECMWF AIFS: Real-time AI global forecasts with high hurricane track skill.
    • Microsoft Aurora: Low-cost global atmospheric & air pollution forecasting.
