AXIOM: THE MATRIX DUALITY

AXIOM is a mathematically complete zero-luck game of linear transformations and group theory played on a 4x4 matrix using a single 52-card deck.

THE DUALITY

  • Alpha (Red Arsenal): Holds 13 Hearts + 13 Diamonds. Dominates Rows.
  • Omega (Black Arsenal): Holds 13 Spades + 13 Clubs. Dominates Columns.

    • ACTIONS (2 AP PER TURN)

  • DEPLOY & AUTO-OVERRIDE: Select any card in hand. Click an empty cell to place it, OR click directly on any lower card to automatically Transcend and override it! (Ace can override any King: the Ouroboros Strike).
  • SUPERPOSE: Place card face-down on an empty cell. Contributes 0 points until observed.
  • OPERATE: Discard a card from hand to trigger its mathematical suit action:
    • ♥ Heart: Swap any 2 cards within the same row.
    • ♦ Diamond: Flip any card on the matrix (Face-Up ↔ Face-Down).
    • ♣ Club: Shift any row or column cyclically (toroidal wrap).
    • ♠ Spade: Banish any face-down card or opponent's card of rank ≤ Spade rank.
  • PROBE: Click on a face-down card to guess its parity (Even/Odd). Correct guess banishes it and draws a card.
  • AUTOMATIC TURN ADVANCEMENT: If you have no cards left in hand, the turn automatically passes so you never have to manually skip turns.

    • VICTORY

  • Eigen-Harmonic Sweep: Control all 4 of your domain lines with at least one Pure Line (all 4 of your color).
  • Sovereign Convergence: Control all 4 Kings face-up simultaneously.
  • Terminal Evaluation: Highest total score when card decks deplete.
